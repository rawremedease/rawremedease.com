# Raw Remedease storefront assets

The menu, logo, and founder photos come from the business's existing [website repository](https://github.com/rawremedease/rawremedease.com/tree/main/images). They are real shop assets. The menu uses the original portrait files where available to show more of each bottle rather than enlarging old thumbnail crops.

| Local asset in `images/` | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `logo.jpg` | Original logo, header, footer and favicon |
| `IMG_3338.jpg`, `IMG_3337-2.jpg`, `IMG_3335-3.jpg` | APB, 14 Carat, Green Light |
| `cinanabutter.jpg`, `IMG_3331-6.jpg`, `IMG_3332-7.jpg` | A Date With Cinanabutter, Serene Green Clean, Dark Knight |
| `IMG_3334-4.jpg`, `IMG_3333-5.jpg` | Seasonal Black Seeded Watermelon, two existing views |
| `watermelon_coconut.jpg`, `berriedtreasure.jpg` | Watermelon & Coconut Water and Berried Treasure |
| `sean.jpg` | Business-supplied founder portrait |
| `house-juices.webp` | AI-assisted editorial still life, styled from the business’s 14 Carat, Green Light and APB reference photos; not a documentary picture of the shop |

DM Sans and DM Serif Display were already part of the shop's identity. The redesign uses the serif rather than the former condensed headline type, and serves Latin-subset WOFF files locally to avoid a font service dependency. The [DM Sans](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/dmsans) and [DM Serif Display](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/dmserifdisplay) licenses are included in `fonts/`.
