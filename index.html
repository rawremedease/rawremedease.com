Our story  /  Southeast D.C.

It started with
a carrot salad.

Sean Smith grew up in Southeast D.C. He’s been a barber, a basketball player, a life coach, a construction worker, and a bartender. It was the bartending that came in handy when he got tired of pouring the juice from his carrot salad down the drain.

He mixed a batch of what became 14 Carat, bottled it, and handed it out at the gym for free. The next time he showed up empty-handed, nobody let him forget it. That was the beginning of Raw Remedease.

For the people, by the people.
