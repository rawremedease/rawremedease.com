Our Mission

Natural Wellness,
From the Earth

Raw Remedease exists to provide Natural RemedEASE — accessible, raw juice crafted for the community. No fillers, no shortcuts. Just the pure power of nature in every sip.

Founded in Washington D.C. by Sean Smith, a former vegan with a passion for plant-based living — Raw Remedease is Black-owned, community-driven, and built for the people. Real ingredients. Real results. Rooted in D.C.

Whether you're looking to boost immunity, improve digestion, detox, or simply feel more vibrant — every bottle is made with intention.
